# Surface Brief: index.html

- **Scope & Mode:** Single-page mobile/tablet field utility (`operate` mode, `code-led` build path).
- **Audience & Job:** Cat owners holding a phone or tablet while searching rooms/under furniture to lure a hiding cat out or prompt a vocal meow reply.
- **Primary Action & Task:** Trigger high-response feline acoustic lures or vocal calls in an automatic Call-and-Listen loop while monitoring quiet intervals for the cat's response and checking off hiding spots.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A handheld feline acoustic caller and listening-window monitor built around the alternating Call-and-Listen cycle, refusing the category default of a static grid of cartoon sound buttons that play continuously and drown out the cat's faint reply.

OWN-WORLD: Gourmet Wet Cat Food Tin Lithography & Coastal Apothecary palette — a Deep Sardine Navy top shell and active transport deck (`#122038`) over Cool Harbor Tin-Pewter ground (`#EEF2F7`), Porcelain cards (`#FFFFFF`), and Slate-Tin secondary panels (`#E2E8F0`), paired with Rich Salmon-Coral (`#B83227`) for active sound emission, Marigold Amber (`#8F5105` / `#E59838`) for the quiet listening window, and Cat-Eye Emerald (`#116647` / `#2CC990`) for detected vocal response spikes, set in clean Thai/Latin UI sans (`Noto Sans Thai` + system UI stack) with tabular numerals (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`). Includes a one-tap Dim-Room / Under-Sofa Torch mode.

STORY: The owner opens the app on their phone or tablet, picks a cat scenario preset (e.g., "ให้ออกมากินขนม" or "ให้ร้องเหมียวตอบรับ") or taps a sound pad, and immediately sees and hears the Call-and-Listen cycle in action: 3 seconds of realistic acoustic lure followed by 4 seconds of silent listening where the live Feline Ear-Perk Spectrum Strip watches for a faint meow or rustle.
